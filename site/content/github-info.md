## Awesome Copilot: example agentic workflows

*Source: [github.com/github/awesome-copilot/workflows](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot/tree/main/workflows)*

Community-contributed workflow recipes you can adapt for your own repositories:

- **daily-issues-report** — generate a daily summary of open issues for your team.
- **ospo-contributors-report** — track open source contributors across your organization.
- **ospo-org-health** — monitor the overall health of your GitHub organization.
- **ospo-release-compliance-checker** — verify releases meet compliance requirements.
- **ospo-stale-repos** — identify repositories that have gone inactive.
- **relevance-check** — assess whether incoming issues or PRs are relevant to your project.
- **relevance-summary** — summarize discussions to quickly surface what matters.
- **weekly-comment-sync** — sync and report on issue/PR comment activity weekly.
